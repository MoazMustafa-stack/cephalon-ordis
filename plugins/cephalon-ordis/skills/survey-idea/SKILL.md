# Survey Idea

Start from the graph's riskiest claims and define the population and decision the survey informs. Ask about past behavior before hypothetical intent. Avoid leading wording, double-barreled questions, and unnecessary personal data. Include screening, disconfirming options, and a short analysis plan. Treat survey output as evidence with provenance, not as validation by itself.
